The Chronicle

A century ago, two houses sealed the citadel to save its people. A rescue bell beneath the stone has put their old agreement to the test.

Timeline Places Leaders

Timeline

  1. The first Accord

    Sun maintained the roads and refuges. Moon kept the civic record. Both seals were required to close the underground vault.

  2. Eclipse night

    The Cinder Road burned as families fled toward the citadel. The Dawnward put those who reached the vault into protective sleep.

  3. The long Watch

    Settlements took root outside the walls. The city’s record called the vault empty, while families along the road kept the names of the missing.

  4. The Watch Beneath the Ash

    A rescue bell rings below the citadel. The Sentinels answer from the sealed stair.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    The refugees see the sky again. Some accounts describe a restored ward lighting the stairs. Others tell of a single lantern passed between the rescuers. One survivor carries a key stamped with both houses’ crests.

  5. The Broken Accord

    The key opens either gate. The infirmary lies beyond Sun Gate and the archives beyond Moon Gate. A Sentinel column is climbing the road.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    At Sun Gate the healers carry the last stretcher through. At Moon Gate the archivists recover a ledger bearing both seals. If the column falls before either gate is secured, the houses lift the bars together.

  6. The Missing Names

    On the Cinder Road, Vael hears a name he carved into his shield a century ago. The party has to get people or evidence past the Watch.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    A living witness can name the keeper who certified the evacuation. The surviving record can establish when the release order was suspended. If neither reaches court, the search for corroboration leads to Stillwater Harbor.

  7. The Tide That Waited

    At Stillwater Harbor, the long-delayed tide begins to turn. The Watch heads for the customs house, where the original release order is kept.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    The party recovers an order naming the local refuges that once had authority to open their own gates. It gives the court a way to challenge the century-old seal.

  8. The Open Accord

    With the original release order in hand, the party returns to court. Both houses sign a new command while the Watch prepares to close the gates again.

    Reveal the campaign ending

    The old seal is lifted. Local refuges recover the authority to open their own doors in an emergency. The court must now settle who directs the repaired Watch and how the settlements outside the walls will be represented.

  9. Keys to the Foundry

    Orrun returns to the Foundry Steps. The workers kept the furnace lit through the long Watch. A court officer arrives for the keys while a Sentinel still holds the door.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    The workers may keep the keys and the right to stop the furnace. The court may take them and read each new order in public. If the party defeats the Watch first, Orrun shuts the furnace and leaves the keys in the lock until the two sides meet.

  10. The Thorn Orchard

    Sylva keeps two beds alive beneath the briars. One feeds the terraces. The other shelters families who may never return to the city. The outcome at the Foundry determines what help reaches her.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    If the beds hold, the shelter opens and the families have food enough to stay or leave on their own terms. If the party stops the Watch first, Sylva returns to count what survived the sweeps.

  11. The Third Seal

    A family brings Neri a gate receipt bearing a seal the court says never existed. The Moon archivist lets the party in before the Watch closes the side door.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    The hearing receives either a ledger with all three seals or testimony from someone who remembers the third. If the party defeats the Watch first, Neri still brings the receipt and asks the court to hear the family that kept it.

  12. The Ash Register

    Neri lays two books on the hearing table. One takes the orders that shut the gates. One takes the names of the people sheltered beneath them. The evidence from the Third Seal determines which book already has a page.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    The party may file both books in open court, where any house can read them, or send copies to the shelters, where families can keep their own account. If the Watch falls first, Neri gathers the books and leaves the question of custody open.

  13. The Doors at Dawn

    The Watch comes at dawn. If the books stayed in court, the clerk has one hearing to read the names. If the shelters have copies, they wait for the party to clear the streets.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    A completed hearing sends the names down the court stairs. If the shelters get their chance to read, people hear them on both sides of the gates. The Watch can clear a room. It cannot take back a name someone has heard.

  14. The Lower Stair

    Someone calls a name Vael etched on his shield. Families wait beneath the grate. The court can send a brace. The shelters can send guides who know the way up.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    If the families reach the top, one of them asks Vael to take a name off his shield. She tells him where to find her now. If the party drives off the Watch first, Vael stays at the chain and listens for the voices below.

  15. The Landing Bell

    Orrun rings the bell above the lower stair. If the families reached the top, the party guards them at the landing. If the Watch fell at the grate first, the party hauls the lift for those still below.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    If the landing holds, the upper door opens and the families hear someone call them in. If the lift reaches the stone, Orrun pins the wheel so everyone can step out at their own pace.

  16. The Upper Door

    Sera opens the street door. The families from the landing can cross under guard. The families from the lift take the service stair, where they need lamps and blankets.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    When the last group crosses the street door, someone asks where to set the kettle. If the service stair is lit, Sera waits at the rail until everyone is through.

  17. The Common Table

    Sera pulls a table into the upper hall. The families from the street door bring chairs for their delegates. The families from the service stair bring their lamps.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    When the delegates are seated, Sera asks who wants to speak first. If the lamps burn through the Watch, the families pull their chairs away from the wall and sit together.

  18. The First Petition

    Sera asks the registry for a door no house can close alone. The delegates can sign in public. The lamplighters can carry copies down the lane while the party keeps the Watch busy.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    If the petition has enough support, the clerk sets it beside the old orders. If the runners get through, a copy reaches every door on the lane before the Watch can close the registry.

  19. The Gate Charter

    The old order comes down from the gatehouse wall. If the petition was signed, the clerk opens a hearing. If the runners made it through, the lane is ready to post the new charter.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    If the hearings hold, Neri pins the charter in place of the old order. If the copies go up across the lane, someone starts reading theirs aloud as the Watch reaches the gatehouse.

  20. The Shared Keys

    Orrun has both keys. If the charter was ratified, two bearers have to keep their keys through the Watch. If copies went up across the lane, the neighbors gather to walk to the gate together.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    If the bars lift, the gate opens toward the road. If the lane rallies, people walk up together and finish the words of the charter when the first reader stops for breath.

  21. The Open Road

    Tala follows the stones out beyond the gate. If the bars lifted, the wagons leave under guard. If the lane rallied, the neighbors move together through the Watch line.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    The last wagon clears the stones, or the last group comes out together. Either way, people start down the road while there is still light.

  22. The Reed Ford

    Iskar knows the stones beneath the water. If the wagons came through, the drivers lay a causeway for their wheels. If the crowd came through on foot, the guides take them into the reeds when the Watch leaves an opening.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    The last wagon reaches the far bank, or the last group comes out of the reeds. Iskar leaves a stone where the next people can see the crossing.

  23. The Far Bank

    Iskar finds dry ground above the ford. The wagons can unload there. The people who came through the shallows wait in the reeds while Tala draws the Watch onto the open bank.

    Reveal chapter aftermath

    The wagon crews set out blankets on the dry shelf. If the last families slip out of the reeds instead, Tala waits until she hears every name called.

The eight leaders

Tala, Ash Rider

She rode the last evacuation road until the stones caught fire. She remembers every gate that stayed closed.

Play style · Fast creatures and attack boosts.

Vael, Dawn Keeper

The names etched inside his shield belong to people he promised to shelter. He is starting to hear those names spoken again.

Play style · Durable blockers, shields, and healing.

Neri, Veil Weaver

She remembers a third seal. The court left its name out of the ledger.

Play style · Removal and flexible reactions.

Sylva, Thorn Regent

She planted her first briar in a burned orchard. Its roots now reach the walls, and the refuge has become a place worth defending.

Play style · Build a wide board with Briar Sprites.

Orrun, Forge Titan

He cast the first Sentinel hinges. He has taken up his hammer again to repair the machines and change the orders they serve.

Play style · Invest mana early to summon heavier threats.

Iskar, Tide Oracle

He saw the eclipse reflected in the harbor a year before it came. When the evacuation boats left, he held the turning tide in place.

Play style · Freeze opposing creatures and draw cards.

Sera, Outer Keeper

Sera never left the outer court. The Sentinels stopped asking her to move.

Play style · Hold the line with blockers, healing, and Silver Veil.

Cass, Unclaimed

Cass came after the eclipse. Both houses asked for a name. Cass gave only one.

Play style · Press with Scouts or build toward Wildfire and Drakes.
