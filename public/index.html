Rules

Choose a leader and play their fixed deck. Start with 20 life and five cards. You may redraw your starting hand once before the host starts.

Mana

On your turn, your mana capacity increases by one, up to ten. Refill it and draw a card. The first player skips their first draw. Unspent mana is available for reactions until your next refill.

Combat

Choose all your attackers, then one opposing player. You get one attack per turn. A new creature can block right away and can normally attack on your next turn. An exhausted creature cannot block.

The defender assigns at most one blocker to each attacker. Players with affordable reactions may each play one or pass. Unblocked attackers hit the player. Blocked creatures deal damage to each other. Surviving creatures recover their HP at the end of the turn.

Leaders

Summon your leader from their own zone. If they fall, you can summon them again. Each summon raises the next cost by one mana.

Trees

Gain one growth point every three turns at the table. Spend points on your turn outside combat. Your first unlock chooses a path for this match. Unlock its nodes in order and read their effects in the tree panel.

Winning

At zero life, after a failed draw, or after conceding, you leave the match. In free-for-all the last player wins. In Teams and Co-op, defeat the opposing side. The story panel explains chapter objectives. In Teams, an offered truce only stops the two players from attacking each other through the end of that turn. If leader damage is enabled, 12 combat damage from the same summoned leader also eliminates a player.
