How to play

  1. Create a room and send its invite link to a friend. Each player starts with 20 life and a shuffled starter deck.
  2. On your turn, draw a card and refill your mana. Your mana capacity grows by one each turn, up to ten. The first player skips their first draw.
  3. Click a card to spend its mana cost. Wildfire asks you to pick an opposing creature or player. Dawnward restores up to four life.
  4. Creatures can attack from your next turn. Select a creature and then its target. Creatures trade damage, which persists. Clear the opposing creatures to attack the player directly.
  5. Win by reducing your opponent to zero life or when they run out of cards to draw.
