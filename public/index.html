How to play

  1. Create a room and share its invite link. Team PvP supports 2v2 and 3v3. Co-op lets one or more allies face Sentinel bots. In either mode, defeat the opposing side to win. You can redraw your opening hand once before the host starts. Choose a leader deck when you enter the room. Each player starts with 20 life and five cards.
  2. On your turn, draw a card and refill your mana. Your mana capacity grows by one each turn, up to ten. The first player skips their first draw.
  3. Click a card to spend its mana cost. Wildfire asks you to pick an opposing creature or player. Dawnward restores up to four life.
  4. Summon your leader from the leader zone. Each summon raises its next cost by one mana. Select all ready creatures you want to attack with, then select one opponent. The defender can assign each available blocker to one attacker. Creature damage clears at the end of each turn.
  5. After blocking, players with an affordable reaction get a chance to protect the defender or strengthen the attacker. Keep mana open to react. Blocking has a 20-second window. Eligible players can react in parallel during the 15-second reaction window. The turn clock pauses during combat.
  6. Players are eliminated at zero life, when they run out of cards to draw, or when they concede. The last player standing wins.
