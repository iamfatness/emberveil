How to play

  1. Create a room and share its invite link. The host starts the match once two to six players have joined. Choose a leader deck when you enter the room. Each player starts with 20 life and five cards.
  2. On your turn, draw a card and refill your mana. Your mana capacity grows by one each turn, up to ten. The first player skips their first draw.
  3. Click a card to spend its mana cost. Wildfire asks you to pick an opposing creature or player. Dawnward restores up to four life.
  4. Summon your leader from the leader zone. Each summon raises its next cost by one mana. Select a ready creature and then an opponent to attack. They can choose one available blocker. Creature damage clears at the end of each turn.
  5. After blocking, players with an affordable reaction get a chance to protect the defender or strengthen the attacker. Keep mana open to react. Blocking and reactions each have a 30-second window.
  6. Players are eliminated at zero life, when they run out of cards to draw, or when they concede. The last player standing wins.
